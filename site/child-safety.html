Child safety standards

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Duhuze has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion and trafficking.

Our standards

  • Duhuze is for users aged 13 and over.
  • Any content or behaviour that sexualises, exploits or endangers children is forbidden (see our terms).
  • Accounts involved in CSAE are removed permanently, and the content is preserved for and reported to the relevant authorities, including Rwanda Investigation Bureau (RIB) and, where applicable, international reporting bodies.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement on lawful requests.

How to report

Email duhuzafro@gmail.com with the username, a description and, if possible, screenshots. Reports about children are handled first. If a child is in immediate danger, call Rwanda's police emergency line or the RIB toll-free line.

Contact for child safety

Child safety point of contact: DUHUZE TECHS, duhuzafro@gmail.com.
