Getting started

Create your account and set Duhuze up the way you like.

Create an account 2 minutes

  1. Open Duhuze and tap Create account.
  2. Sign up with your email and a password, Continue with Google, or Continue with phone (we send you a code by SMS).
  3. Complete your profile: a photo, your name, a short bio and a unique username so friends can find you.
  4. A short welcome tour shows you the main things. You can skip it.
Duhuze is for people aged 13 and over.

Language: English or Kinyarwanda

Go to Settings → Language and choose Kinyarwanda or English. The whole app changes at once.

Lite mode: save your data bundle Good on 3G

Open Settings → Lite mode (save data) and choose Auto (turns on by itself when you use mobile data), On or Off. In Lite mode, videos in the feed wait until you tap ▶ instead of downloading by themselves.

Videos you upload from a phone are made lighter before they are sent, so uploads finish faster on 3G.

Dark or light theme

Settings → Theme Mode switches between a dark and a light look.

Home feed & reels

Three ways to scroll, and everything you can do with a post.

For You, Reels and Following

  • For You: popular and fresh posts from all of Duhuze, chosen for you. The feed opens here.
  • Reels: videos only, full screen. Swipe up for the next one; on a PC use the ↑ ↓ keys or the arrow buttons.
  • Following: only the people and pages you follow, newest first. If it's empty, Duhuze suggests pages to follow.
Pull down (or press F5 on a PC) to refresh.

Like, comment, save and share

  • Like: tap ♥, or double-tap the photo.
  • Comment: tap 💬 and write. You can like comments too.
  • Save: tap 🔖 to keep the post in Saved.
  • Share: tap ✈️ (see Sharing).
  • Message: the Message button next to the name opens a chat with the creator.
  • ⋯ menu: Share, Copy link, Report, or Block the person.

Posts with several photos or videos show dots (on a PC, arrows and a counter like 2/5). Photos are always shown whole, never cropped.

The blue ✓

A blue ✓ next to a name means the Duhuze team checked that this is the real person or business. See Getting verified.

Creating posts

Photos, videos and words, as yourself or as your page.

Make a post

  1. Tap ＋ Create → Create post.
  2. Add photos and/or videos (several at once), or write text only.
  3. For videos, pick a cover picture.
  4. Write a caption. Tap 😊 for emojis.
  5. Choose who posts: you or one of your pages.
  6. Tap Post. Big photos are made lighter automatically.

Sell from a post: tag a product Pages

When you post as a page that has a shop, choose Tag a product. Your post then shows Buy · 5,000 RWF; buyers tap it and land in your shop with that product already in the cart.

#Hashtags and @mentions New

Write #kigali, #food… in a post: the tag turns blue and anyone can tap it to see every post with that tag (or search #kigali in Discover).

Write @ and the start of a username in a post or a comment: Duhuze suggests people, tap one to mention them. They get a notification and can tap through to the post. Links (https://…) are tappable too.

Edit or delete your post

Tap ⋯ on your own post → Edit, Delete or Boost post.

Stories (status)

Moments that disappear after 24 hours.

Share a status

  1. At the top of the feed, tap your circle with ＋.
  2. Write coloured text, or pick a photo or a video (up to 60 seconds).
  3. Add a caption and tap Send. It disappears after 24 hours.

Edit your photo: filters, text and stickers New

  • Filters: Vivid, Warm, Cool, B&W, Vintage, Fade.
  • Aa Text: add words, drag them anywhere, tap to change colour or put them on a box.
  • 😊 Stickers: emoji you can drag and resize (pinch, or the mouse wheel on a PC).
  • Long-press (right-click on a PC) a sticker or text to remove it.

Watch, react and reply

Tap a circle to watch. Tap the right side for next, the left for back, and hold to pause (on a PC: ← →, Space, Esc). Send a quick reaction ❤️ 😂 😮 😢 🔥 👏 or type a reply; it arrives in your chat with that person. On your own status you can see who viewed it.

Sharing

Send posts to friends, groups, your story or any app.

The share menu

Tap ✈️ on any post:

  • Send to: pick one or many chats or groups (🔍 to find anyone), add a message, and send. It arrives as a post card they can tap.
  • Add to story: the post becomes your status for 24 hours, with a View post button.
  • Copy link: a link like duhuze-rw.web.app/p/…. Anyone can open it; with the app installed it opens in Duhuze.
  • Share to…: WhatsApp, Telegram, email and other apps on your phone or PC.

Discover & search

Find people, pages, posts and places.

Search

Tap Discover (on a PC Ctrl+K) and type a name, a business type or a word. Switch between All, Pages, People and Posts.

For pages, narrow it down with filters:

  • Category (food, fashion, salons…)
  • 📍 Place: Kigali, Musanze, Kimironko…
  • 🛍 Has a shop, ✓ Verified, ⭐ 4+ stars, 🕒 Open now

Follow

Follow people and pages to see their posts in Following. Your followers and who you follow are on your profile.

Chat

Private conversations with everything you need.

Messages, photos, voice notes and files

Open Messages, then a chat. You can send:

  • Text with emojis 😊 and replies to a specific message (long-press a message)
  • Photos and videos, documents (PDF, Word…), and voice notes (🎤)
  • Your location once, or live location for 15 minutes, 1 hour or 8 hours (stop any time)

You can edit or delete your messages. You see when someone is online or typing…. Media shows all photos, videos, files and links shared in the chat.

Audio and video calls

Tap 📞 or 🎥 at the top of a chat. The call goes directly between the two devices where possible.

Ghost messages 👻

Turn on ghost mode in a chat (or by default in Settings). Ghost messages disappear after they are read

Message requests, archive and clearing

  • People you don't know land in Requests: Approve to chat, or Decline.
  • Archive a chat to hide it from your inbox (find it in Archived).
  • Clear chat removes the messages for you only.
  • Inbox filters: All, Unread, Groups, Creators, Archived, Requests.

Group chats

Chat with many people, with admins and settings.

Create a group

  1. In Messages, tap 👥＋ New group.
  2. Pick members (people you follow and who follow you come first, or search).
  3. Give it a name, a photo and a description → Create group.

Tap the group name at the top of the chat to open Group info.

Admins and members Up to 256 people

WhoCan do
Owner (creator)Everything, including removing admins. Can't be removed.
AdminsAdd and remove members, make others admin, change name/photo/description, settings, invite link.
MembersChat, see members, leave.

If the owner leaves, another admin becomes the owner. Changes appear in the chat as small grey lines, like "Alice added Bob".

Invite with a link

In Group info, admins copy the invite link (duhuze-rw.web.app/g/…) and send it anywhere. To join, open the link or go to Messages → 🔗 Join a group and paste it. Admins can reset the link so the old one stops working.

Group settings

  • Only admins can send messages: good for announcement groups.
  • Only admins can edit group info.

Pages for businesses & creators

Your public page, your followers, your brand.

Create a page First page free

  1. Open My pages → New page (or ＋ Create → Create page).
  2. Add the name, logo, category, bio, address (and a Google Maps link), phone and email.
  3. Optional: your MoMo Pay merchant code and the name registered on MoMo Pay, to sell in your shop.

Your first page is free. Want a second page (another business or brand)? Each extra page is paid once (5,000 RWF) and is yours for good. My pages shows how many you use, for example 1 of 1, and Get more adds a place.

Payments for extra pages and boosts go through IremboPay (MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, cards). This is being connected now: until it's live the app says "Payments are coming soon" and nothing is charged.

Opening hours New

Edit your page → Opening hours → set the times for each day (or Open 24/7). Visitors then see Open now · until 18:00 or Closed · opens 08:00 on your page, on the map and in Discover, where people can filter by 🕒 Open now.

Hide or show a page

My pages → ⋮ → Hide page takes it off Duhuze and frees its place. To bring it back, tap 👁 (show hidden pages) → Show again; this needs a free place, like creating a page.

Page team: run a page together New

Open My pages → ⋮ → Page team → Add person, type their @username and pick a role:

RoleCan do
👑 OwnerEverything, including the team, verification and hiding the page
🛡 AdminEdit the page and logo, post as the page, run the shop, see and handle orders
✏️ EditorPost as the page

Up to 10 people. They get a notification, and the page appears in their My pages under Pages you help run. Anyone can leave a team from there.

Boost a post Reach more people

On your post tap ⋯ → Boost post, pick 3, 7 or 14 days (2,000 / 4,000 / 7,000 RWF), then pay. The post shows near the top of For You for everyone, marked Sponsored. Boosting again adds days after the current boost ends.

What visitors see on your page

  • Your posts in a neat grid
  • 🔥 Popular in this shop: your best-selling products first
  • Reviews: ⭐ 1–5 stars and comments (one per person; you can't review your own page)
  • Buttons to follow, message and open the shop

Insights

Insights shows your posts, likes and comments per post, activity over the last 7 days, unread chats and notifications, and your saved items.

Shops, payments & orders

Sell and buy with MTN MoMo, and follow every order.

For sellers: open your shop

  1. Add your MoMo Pay merchant code and the name registered on MoMo Pay to your page (edit your page).
  2. Open My shop → Add product: name, price in RWF, photo, in stock.
  3. Tag products in your posts so people can tap Buy.

For buyers: buy and pay

  1. Open a shop (🛍 Shop on a post or page, or Buy on a tagged post).
  2. Choose quantities and tap Pay … with MoMo.
  3. On a phone, Duhuze opens the MTN MoMo dial screen with the shop's code and the amount filled in (*182*8*1*…#). Enter your PIN in MoMo. On a PC, Duhuze shows the code to dial on your phone.
  4. Come back to Duhuze and tap I have dialled it, adding the MoMo transaction ID from your SMS if you have it.
Duhuze never sees your MoMo PIN and never holds your money: you pay the shop directly through MTN.
Tapped twice by mistake? No problem: the same checkout always gives the same order, so you are never charged for a double order.

Follow your order

OrderedPaidConfirmedReadyDelivered

You get a notification at every step. Sellers move orders forward from Orders: check the MoMo SMS, then Received ✓, later Ready and Mark delivered. If the money didn't arrive, the seller taps Not received and you can check and pay again.

Getting verified

The blue ✓ for real people and real businesses.

How to ask

For your account: Settings → Get verified. For a page: open your page → Get verified. Duhuze shows a checklist; when every item is ✓ you can send the request, and the team reviews it.

The checklist shows your progress (for example 6 of 10 done), your numbers next to each item, and what to do for the ones that are missing.

Notifications, profile & settings

Stay up to date and in control of your account.

Notifications

The 🔔 shows likes, comments, new followers, messages, message requests, order updates, reviews and messages from the Duhuze team. Filter them by type, and mark them all as read.

Your profile and saved posts

Your profile shows your posts, followers, following and pages. Edit Profile changes your photo, name, bio and contact details. Saved keeps the posts you bookmarked.

Delete your account

Settings → Delete Account removes your profile, posts, pages, products, statuses, comments, likes, follows, notifications, photos and videos, and your login. Messages you sent stay in the other person's chat, like in other messaging apps. More on the Delete account page.

Safety & reporting

Keeping Duhuze safe for everyone.

Report and block

  • Report a post, a status, a chat or a group: tap ⋯ (or Report or block in a chat) and choose the reason, for example spam, harassment, a scam or child safety.
  • Block someone: they can't message you and you won't see their posts.

The Duhuze team reviews reports (child-safety reports first, immediately). Content that breaks the rules is removed, and accounts or pages can be suspended. A suspended page disappears from feeds and search and its shop stops taking orders.

If a child is in danger, report it in the app and contact Rwanda Investigation Bureau (RIB) at once. See our Child safety standards.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Is Duhuze free?
Yes. Creating an account, posting, chatting and your first page are free. Only two things are paid, and only if you want them: an extra page (paid once) and boosting a post. When you buy from a shop you pay the shop directly with MTN MoMo.
What does "Sponsored" mean on a post?
The owner paid to show that post to more people in For You for a few days. It follows the same rules as every post; you can report it like any other.
Why do I have to pay for a second page?
Everyone gets one page free. Paying once for each extra page keeps Duhuze free of fake and copy pages, and helps keep Duhuze running. Hiding a page frees its place.
Does Duhuze see my MoMo PIN or keep my money?
No. You type your PIN only in MTN Mobile Money. Duhuze never sees it and never holds money; it only records the order and, if you type it, the transaction ID.
I paid but the shop says "Not received". What now?
Check the MoMo SMS: the amount and the transaction ID. Open the order and tap Check & pay again with the right ID, or message the shop. If you still disagree, report the order; the Duhuze team can see both sides.
Why can't I see someone's posts any more?
You may have blocked each other, or their account or page was suspended for breaking the rules.
How do I use less data?
Turn on Settings → Lite mode. Videos then wait for a tap, and photos are lighter.
Who can see my location?
Only the people you send it to in a chat, and only when you choose. Live location stops when the time you picked ends or when you stop it.
Are ghost messages really gone?
They disappear from the chat after they are read and can never be seen again.
How do I get the blue ✓?
Meet the checklist in Settings → Get verified (or on your page), then send the request. The team checks every one.
Can I use Duhuze on my computer?
Yes: Duhuze for Windows has the same account, chats and feed, plus keyboard shortcuts.
How do I delete my account?
In the app: Settings → Delete Account. Or write to us, see Delete account.
