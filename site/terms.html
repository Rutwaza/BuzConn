1. Your account

You must be 13 or older. Keep your login safe; you are responsible for what happens in your account. One person, one personal account.

2. What you may not post or do

  • Anything illegal in Rwanda, including hate speech, genocide ideology and incitement to violence.
  • Sexual content involving minors, or any content that exploits or endangers children (we report it to the authorities).
  • Harassment, threats, bullying, sharing other people's private information or intimate images.
  • Scams, fake shops, fake payments, impersonation, spam.

We may remove content and suspend or delete accounts that break these rules. You can report content or users to duhuzafro@gmail.com.

3. Your content

You keep ownership of what you post. You allow Duhuze to store, show and send it to other users so the service works.

4. Shops and payments

Businesses sell their own products and receive money directly through MTN MoMo Pay. Duhuze is not a party to the sale, does not hold money and cannot refund payments. Check the name MTN shows before entering your PIN. Disputes are between the buyer and the shop; for wrong MoMo payments contact MTN.

4b. Paid features (extra pages, boosts)

Your first page is free. An extra page is paid once; a boost shows a post as "Sponsored" in For You for the days you choose. Prices are shown before you pay, in RWF, and payments are handled by IremboPay. A paid page place stays yours while your account is active. A boosted post must follow these terms like any post: if it is removed for breaking them, the boost ends and is not refunded. If a payment was taken but the item was not given, write to us and we will fix it.

5. Location sharing

Share your location only with people you trust. You can stop live location at any time.
