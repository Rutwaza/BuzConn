Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Duhuze ("we") collects, why, where it is kept and what your rights are. Duhuze is run by DUHUZE TECHS, Rwanda. Contact: duhuzafro@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

  • Account: name, username, email address and/or phone number, profile photo, bio, and how you sign in (email, Google or phone).
  • What you share: posts, photos, videos, reels, statuses, comments, likes, follows, business pages and shop products.
  • Messages: chats, photos, voice notes, documents and calls you make in the app. Call signalling passes through our servers; the call audio/video goes directly between the phones where possible.
  • Ghost messages: they disappear from the chat after they are read, but we keep an encrypted copy. It can only be opened by the Duhuze administrator for a safety report (for example abuse, threats or fraud) or a lawful request from authorities, and every opening is logged.
  • Location: only when you choose to. When you send your location in a chat, or share your live location (it keeps updating, also in the background with a notification, until the time you chose or until you stop it). Businesses may add their address to their page. We do not track your location otherwise.
  • Shop orders: what you bought, the amount, the shop's MoMo Pay code and, if you type it, the MoMo transaction ID. Payment itself happens in MTN Mobile Money; we never see your MoMo PIN and we do not hold money.
  • Technical data: a push-notification token for your device, whether you are online and typing (shown to the people you chat with), app settings (language, theme, lite mode). We do not use advertising IDs, ads or analytics trackers.

2. Why we use it

  • To run the service: show your profile and posts, deliver messages and notifications, run feeds and search, process shop orders.
  • To keep people safe: investigate reports of abuse, fraud or illegal content, and respond to lawful requests.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who sees it

  • Other users: your profile, posts, statuses, pages and comments are public to Duhuze users. Messages are only for the people in the chat.
  • Service providers that store and process data for us: Google Firebase (accounts, database, notifications; servers in the United States), Cloudflare R2 (photos and videos), OpenStreetMap (map pictures in chat: your phone loads map tiles from their servers).
  • The Duhuze administrator: can open a chat or ghost copies only to investigate a report (abuse, threats, fraud, child safety) or answer a lawful request. Each opening is logged.
  • Authorities: only when the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

  • Statuses: 24 hours, then deleted.
  • Your account data: until you delete your account (see below).
  • Ghost message copies: while your account exists, and 90 days after you delete it, then deleted.
  • Shop orders: kept for the shop's sales records.

5. Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete Account. This deletes your profile, posts, pages, shop products, statuses, comments, likes, follows, notifications, your photos and videos, and your login. Messages you sent stay in the other person's chat (like other messaging apps). You can also ask us by email: see Delete your account.

6. Your rights

Under Rwanda's law on the protection of personal data and privacy you can ask to see your data, correct it, delete it, object to its use, or get a copy. Write to duhuzafro@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. You may also complain to the National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA).

7. Children

Duhuze is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from younger children; if you think a child under 13 has an account, tell us and we will delete it. See our child safety standards.

8. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Access to the database is controlled by strict security rules; ghost copies are encrypted with AES-256. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at once if you notice a problem.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and tell you in the app when the change is important.
