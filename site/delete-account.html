Delete your Duhuze account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Duhuze and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm with your password, or type DELETE (Google/phone accounts: sign out and in again first if the app asks).

Without the app

Send an email to CHANGE_ME_EMAIL from the email address of your account, or tell us your username and phone number. We confirm it is you and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Deleted: profile, username, posts, reels, statuses, business pages and shop products, comments, likes, follows, notifications, your photos and videos, and your login.

Kept: messages you sent stay in the other person's chat; shop orders stay in the shop's sales records; encrypted ghost-message copies are kept 90 days for safety reports, then deleted.
