For businesses

Your shop, right where people scroll

Open a free page, add your products and your MoMo Pay code, and start selling.

  • Buy buttons on your posts. Tag a product; customers tap Buy · price and order.
  • Paid straight to your MoMo. Duhuze never holds the money and never sees PINs.
  • Orders you can follow. Mark Ready and Delivered; your customer is notified.
  • Reviews and best sellers. Stars and "Popular in this shop" build trust.
  • The blue ✓. Ask to get verified once your page is established.
