SpotLight v1.0.0

SpotLight is now live with a more social, creator-focused experience across the app.

What's new
- A redesigned Spotlight feed with smarter ranking, highlights, smoother interactions, and richer media posts

- Improved post details and comments with threaded replies, likes, better media layout, and stronger social polish

- Release build support updated for notification-related dependencies
